After a recording, Moxie writes enhanced notes from the transcript on this Mac. Your own notes stay in **My notes**.

1. Open the note.
2. Click **Enhanced** to read the enhanced notes. Click **My notes** to go back.
3. To change the template, click the template name adjacent to **Redo**, for example **General**.
4. Select a template. Moxie writes the enhanced notes again.

Click **Redo** to write the enhanced notes again with the same template.

If you changed the enhanced notes, Moxie asks **Replace your edited notes?**. Click **Replace** or **Keep my edits**.

To let Moxie use your own notes also, set **Settings → AI features → Should enhanced notes use your own notes?** to on.

## Templates

| Group | Templates |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | General, Brief, Detailed, Key quotes |
| **Team** | Team meeting, Planning and kickoff, Retrospective, Brainstorm |
| **People** | One-on-one, Job interview |
| **Customers** | Customer call, User interview |

The **Recent** group shows the last 3 templates that you used.

## Example

The **General** template gives these sections:

```text
Summary
Decisions
Open questions
Action items and next steps
```

The statements have citations. Click a citation to open the transcript at that line.
