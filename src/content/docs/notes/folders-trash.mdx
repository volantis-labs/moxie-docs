---
title: Folders and Trash
description: Put notes in folders, move notes to Trash and restore them.
---

Folders keep your notes in groups. Trash keeps a deleted note for 30 days.

## Put a note in a folder

1. In the sidebar, below **Folders**, click **New folder** (+).
2. Type a name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
3. Open a note.
4. At the top of the note, click **No folder**.
5. Select the folder.

Click a folder in the sidebar to see its notes. The folder page has **Rename** and **Delete folder**. To delete a folder, click **Delete folder**, then click **Delete folder for good?**. Moxie keeps the notes.

## Delete and restore a note

1. Open the note.
2. Click **Move to Trash**.
3. To get the note back, click **Trash** in the sidebar, then click **Restore**.

To delete a note immediately, click **Delete forever** in **Trash**, then click **Delete for good?**. Moxie does not change exported files, Moxie backups or Time Machine copies.

After 30 days in **Trash**, Moxie deletes the note for good. You cannot change this time.

## Example

Each row in **Trash** shows the age of the deletion:

```text
Trashed 3 days ago
```
