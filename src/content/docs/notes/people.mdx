---
title: People
description: Open the page of a person to see their mentions and meetings.
---

Moxie keeps a page for each person in your meetings. The page shows the notes that mention the person, and the meetings of the person.

Moxie adds a person from these sources: calendar attendees, speakers that have a name, names that you type after `@`, and your sign-in account.

## Open a person page

| Where | Action |
| --- | --- |
| A note | Click an `@` mention |
| The transcript pane | Click the row of people, then click a name |
| Search (<kbd>⌘K</kbd>) | Type a name or an email, then select the person below **People** |

The page is read-only. It shows the name, the email if Moxie knows it, **Mentions** and **Meetings**. The newest rows are first.

1. Click a row below **Mentions**. Moxie opens the note at that line.
2. Click a row below **Meetings**. Moxie opens the note of that meeting.

Notes in Trash do not show on the page.

## Example

A note has this line:

```text
Decision: @Dana sends the budget on Friday.
```

Click **@Dana**. The page of Dana shows this line below **Mentions**.
