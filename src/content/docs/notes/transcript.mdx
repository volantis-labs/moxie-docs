---
title: Live transcript
description: Read and search the transcript while Moxie records.
---

During a recording, the transcript pane of the note shows the words while the participants speak.

1. Open the note of the recording. The transcript pane opens on the right side.
2. Read the lines. Each line has a time and a speaker.
3. To find a word, type it in **Search transcript**.
4. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to go to the next match.

Before the first words, the pane shows **Listening…**. The pane keeps the newest line in view. Scroll up to read earlier lines.

| Press | Result |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>⇧⌘\\</kbd> | Open or close the transcript pane |
| <kbd>⌘F</kbd>, with the cursor in the pane | Go to **Search transcript** |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Next match |
| <kbd>⇧Enter</kbd> | Previous match |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Clear the search |

## Example

```text
00:42  Me      Let us look at the plan.
00:47  Others  The first item is the budget.
```

**Me** is your microphone. **Others** is the meeting sound. After the meeting, Moxie divides **Others** into speakers. The transcript stays in the note.
