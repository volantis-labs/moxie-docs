Chat answers a question from your notes and transcripts. The model runs on your Mac. Moxie does not save the conversation.

## Ask about one note

1. Open a note.
2. Type a question in the **Ask about this note** field.
3. Press <kbd>Return</kbd>.

Moxie also shows a suggested question. Click the question to ask it.

## Ask across all notes

1. Click **Chat** in the sidebar.
2. Type a question in the **Ask across your notes** field.
3. Press <kbd>Return</kbd>.

Click **New conversation** to start again.

## Read the answer

| In the answer | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| A number | Shows the quote from the transcript |
| **Show in transcript** | Opens the transcript at that line |
| **Follow up** | Shows questions that you can ask next |
| **Copy with quote** | Copies the answer and its quotes |

Click **Stop** to stop an answer.

## Ask during a meeting

While Moxie records, the field shows **Ask about this meeting**. Moxie answers from the transcript up to that moment. Only you see the answer. Moxie does not save it.

## Example

```text
What was decided in this meeting?
```

Moxie gives the decisions. A number after a statement is a citation. Click the number to see the line from the transcript.
