A citation is a small link after a sentence in a note. It shows a time or a number. It opens the transcript at the line that the sentence comes from.

## Open a citation

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Move the pointer to a citation | A card shows the transcript line. **Show in transcript** opens the transcript. |
| Click a citation | The transcript pane opens at that line |
| Select a citation with the arrow keys, then press <kbd>Enter</kbd> | The transcript pane opens at that line |

## Add a citation with Find in transcript

1. Select a sentence in the note. A menu shows near the selection.
2. Click **Find in transcript**. A list shows a maximum of 3 transcript lines, each with the speaker and the time.
3. Click the correct line.

Moxie adds a citation after the selection. The transcript pane opens at that line.

- If no line agrees with the sentence, the list shows **No matching line**.
- The **Find in transcript** button shows only when the note has a transcript.

## Example

Select "Sam sends the budget on Friday". Click **Find in transcript**. Click the line "Sam · 12:04". The note shows the citation "12:04" after the sentence.
