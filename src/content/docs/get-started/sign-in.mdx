---
title: Sign in and connect your calendar
description: Sign in with Google or Microsoft to use Moxie and to connect your calendar.
---

Sign in with a Google account or a Microsoft account before you use Moxie. The same step connects your calendar.

1. Open Moxie. At the first start, the **Sign in to Moxie** screen shows after the language screen.
2. Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.
3. Sign in to your account in your browser.
4. Allow access to your calendars. Moxie cannot complete the sign-in without this access.
5. Go back to Moxie.

Moxie only reads your calendar. It cannot change your calendar. Moxie uses your events to show your next meetings, to give titles to notes and to suggest speaker names. Your calendar events stay on your Mac.

You can sign in with only one account at a time. To use a different account, sign out first.

A Microsoft 365 work account can need an approval from your company. If Moxie shows **Ask your IT admin**, ask your IT admin to allow Moxie. Then sign in again.

## Example

Use this path to see your account, to select calendars or to sign out:

**Settings → General → Calendar account**

- **Show calendar choices** shows a switch for each calendar.
- **Sign out** removes your account from Moxie.
