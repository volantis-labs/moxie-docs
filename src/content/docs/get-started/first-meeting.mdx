Record one meeting. Moxie then writes the transcript, names the speakers and makes enhanced notes.

1. Join your meeting. Moxie shows **Meeting detected** and a countdown.
2. Wait until the countdown stops, or click **Start now**. To record nothing, click **Not this meeting**.
3. Tell the other persons that you transcribe the meeting. To copy a text for the meeting chat, click **Copy notice**.
4. Have your meeting. The recording control shows the time and two sound meters.
5. At the end of the meeting, click **Stop**.
6. Open Moxie, then open the note of the meeting.

If Moxie does not detect the meeting, click **New note** on **Home**. Then click **Start capture** in the note.

Moxie works on the note after the recording stops. You can read the note during this time.

## Example

While Moxie works, the note shows a line such as this:

```text
Processing: step 2 of 7 · Speakers
```

When the work is complete, use **My notes** and **Enhanced** to change between your notes and the enhanced notes.
