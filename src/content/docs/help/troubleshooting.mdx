---
title: Troubleshooting
description: What to do when Moxie shows a problem.
---

Find the message that Moxie shows, then do the action.

## During a recording

The recording control shows these messages.

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Your microphone is silent** | If you muted your microphone in the meeting app, do nothing. If not, click **Choose microphone**. |
| **Meeting sound not heard** | Click **Check output**. Make sure that the meeting plays through the selected sound output. |
| **Device changed to** and a device name | Click **OK**. The recording continues. |
| **Disk almost full** | Click **Free up space**. Delete files that you do not need. |
| **Microphone permission removed** | Click **Open Settings**. Turn on Moxie. |
| **System audio permission turned off** | Click **Open System Settings**. Turn on Moxie. |

## Before or after a recording

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Download interrupted** | Click **Retry**. Make sure that your Mac has a network connection and free disk space. |
| **Moxie needs calendar access** | Sign in again. Allow access to your calendars. |
| **Ask your IT admin** | Ask your IT admin to allow Moxie. Then sign in again. |
| **Sign-in expired.** | Go to **Settings → General → Calendar account**. Click **Reconnect**. |

For a missing permission, go to **Settings → Recording** and click **Allow**.

## Example

You connect headphones during a meeting. The recording control shows this line:

```text
Device changed to AirPods Pro    OK
```
