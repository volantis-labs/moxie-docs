---
title: In-person meetings
description: Record a meeting in a room with the microphone of your Mac.
---

Moxie can record a meeting in a room. In-person mode separates the voices that your microphone hears.

1. Start a recording yourself. Refer to [Start and stop a recording](/moxie/docs/record/start-stop/).
2. In the transcript pane, click **More** (…) adjacent to **Stop**.
3. Make sure that **In person meeting** has a check mark. If it has no check mark, click it.

If no meeting app is in use when you start, Moxie sets in-person mode to on automatically.

The menu bar has the same item. During the recording, right-click the Moxie icon, then click **In person meeting**.

## What changes

| Item | In-person mode |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker labels | **Person 1**, **Person 2** and so on |
| Your voice | **Me**, if you have a voice profile |
| Meeting sound | Moxie gives no alert when it hears no meeting sound |

## Example

```text
Me        Let us start with the budget.
Person 1  The numbers are in the sheet.
Person 2  I have one question.
```

The labels can change after the meeting, when Moxie processes the note. Click a label to give the speaker a name.
