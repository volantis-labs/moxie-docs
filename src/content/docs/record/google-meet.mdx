---
title: Google Meet extension
description: Let Moxie get speaker names and slides from Google Meet in Chrome or Edge.
---

The **Moxie for Google Meet** extension for Chrome and Edge tells Moxie who speaks in a Google Meet meeting.

Moxie records and transcribes Google Meet without the extension. The extension adds this data:

| The extension reads | The note gets |
| --- | --- |
| The names on the participant tiles, and who speaks | Speaker names |
| Slides that other participants present | Screen captures of the slides |

The extension does not read the chat or the captions. It sends the data only to the Moxie app on this Mac.

## Check the connection

1. Open **Settings → Recording**.
2. Find **Do you use Google Meet?**.
3. Read the status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The extension connected to Moxie. |
| **Not installed** | No extension connected to Moxie. |

The extension connects when you open a Google Meet page in Chrome or Edge.

If the row shows **Install for Chrome or Edge**, click it. Moxie opens the store page of the extension.

## Example

You open a Google Meet page in Chrome, then you open **Settings → Recording**. The status below **Do you use Google Meet?** is **Connected**.
