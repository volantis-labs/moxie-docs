Moxie records your microphone and the meeting sound. The transcript goes into a note.

## Start

Moxie detects a meeting in Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet. Then a countdown shows on the recording control, or at the top of the Moxie window:

```text
Meeting detected · starts in 10 s
```

| Click | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing | The recording starts after the countdown. |
| **Start now** | The recording starts immediately. |
| **Not this meeting** | Moxie does not record this meeting. |

To start a recording yourself, click **Start capture** in one of these places:

- On **Home**, on the card of your next meeting.
- In a note that has no recording.
- In the menu of the Moxie icon in the menu bar.

## Stop

Click **Stop** in the transcript pane of the note, or on the recording control. Moxie saves the recording.

At the end of a detected meeting, Moxie shows this line:

```text
Meeting ended · stopping in 10 s
```

Click **Keep recording** to continue.

## Example

To get a question as an alternative to the countdown:

1. Open **Settings → Recording**.
2. Below **When a meeting starts, what should Moxie do?**, select **Ask me**.

Moxie then shows **Meeting detected** and waits for **Start**. The other values are **Count down 10 s** (the default) and **Count down 5 s**.
