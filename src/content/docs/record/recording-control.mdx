During a recording, a small control stays above your other apps. It shows the time, a meter for your microphone, a meter for the meeting sound, and **Stop**.

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Click **Stop** | Moxie stops and saves the recording. Moxie does not come to the front. |
| Click the body of the control | Moxie opens the note of the recording. |
| Drag the control | The control moves. Moxie remembers the position for each display. |

The control does not show in a screen share or in a screenshot.

When the Moxie window is in front, the control hides. The transcript pane of the note then shows the same data. If the pane is closed, a **Meeting in progress** line at the top has **Open note** and **Stop**.

## Problems

When there is a problem, the control shows one line of text and one button.

| Text | Button |
| --- | --- |
| Your microphone is silent | **Choose microphone** |
| Meeting sound not heard | **Check output** |
| Disk almost full | **Free up space** |
| Device changed to AirPods | **OK** |

## Example

You share your screen in Zoom. The participants do not see the control. At the end, click **Stop** on the control. Zoom stays in front.
