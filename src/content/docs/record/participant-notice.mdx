---
title: Tell participants
description: Copy a notice line and paste it into the meeting chat.
---

Moxie gives you one line of text that tells the participants about the transcription. You paste the line into the chat of the meeting.

1. Copy the notice. Use one of the places in the table.
2. Go to the chat of your meeting app.
3. Press <kbd>⌘V</kbd>.

| Where | When | Click |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The start prompt | Before the recording starts | **Copy notice** |
| The transcript pane | During the recording | **More** (…), then **Copy participant notice** |
| The Moxie icon in the menu bar | During the recording | Right-click, then **Copy participant notice** |

## Example

Moxie copies this line, in the language of the app:

```text
I’m using Moxie to transcribe this meeting on my computer. Nothing leaves my machine.
```

The note then shows the time of the copy below its title:

```text
Participant notice copied at 10:02
```

An export of the note includes this record.
