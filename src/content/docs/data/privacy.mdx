Moxie makes the transcript, the speaker names and the summaries on your Mac. Moxie does not send your meeting content from your Mac.

## What stays on your Mac

| Data | What Moxie does |
| --- | --- |
| Audio | Moxie keeps the audio only until it processes the note. Then Moxie deletes the audio. If a step fails, Moxie keeps the audio for a retry. |
| Transcripts, notes, summaries, speaker names, screen text | Moxie stores them on your Mac. |
| Calendar events | Moxie reads your calendar read-only and stores the events on your Mac. |

## Protect the notes on disk

Moxie does not encrypt its files. FileVault, a function of macOS, encrypts the disk of your Mac.

If FileVault is off, **Settings → Your data** shows **FileVault is off**. Click **Check FileVault** to open the FileVault settings of macOS.

## When Moxie uses the network

| Use | When |
| --- | --- |
| Model downloads | During setup, and when you download a model again |
| Update checks | While Moxie is open |
| Calendar | While a Google or Microsoft account is connected |
| Diagnostics | When diagnostics are on. See [Diagnostics](/moxie/docs/data/diagnostics/). |
| Work email form | Only if you submit **Share work email…** |

When you delete a note, Moxie does not delete your exported files, backups or Time Machine copies.

## Example

To see the full notice in the app, go to **Settings → Your data → Privacy notice**.
